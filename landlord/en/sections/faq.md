---
title: "❓ Frequently Asked Questions"
items:
  - question: 'Q: Is "Landlord Support Service" the same as property management?'
    answer: >-
      A: We prefer "support" because it accurately reflects our role: we
      assist with operations, coordination, and communication—while you
      retain full ownership control. For activities requiring a real estate
      license (like leasing or tenant placement), we partner with licensed
      agents.
  - question: "Q: Can you advertise my property to find tenants?"
    answer: >-
      A: IdealHub does not advertise rentals or place tenants. However, if
      you'd like help finding a tenant, we can connect you with a licensed
      agent in our network who specializes in your area.
