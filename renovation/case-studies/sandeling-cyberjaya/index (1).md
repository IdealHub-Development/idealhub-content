## The Project Story

Sanderling Lakefront Residence located at Cyberjaya, this condominium was transformed into a serene and personal **contemporary classic** sanctuary for the owner's own stay. Designed to balance everyday comfort with timeless elegance, the home was thoughtfully crafted within an RM65,000 renovation budget.

Soft cream wainscoting frames the living room and bedroom walls, introducing structural sophistication and visual warmth. In the main lounge, a striking dark walnut fluted feature wall with a floating console serves as a modern focal point, grounding the space with rich contrast. Beveled diamond-grid accent mirrors in the dining area enhance the sense of space and light, while soft neutral furnishings complete a tranquil setting tailored for relaxed everyday living.
