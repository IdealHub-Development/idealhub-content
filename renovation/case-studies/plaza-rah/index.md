### The Project Story

Located in the vibrant enclave of Kampung Baru, this unit at Plaza Rah underwent a complete cosmetic and functional refurbishment to transform an aging layout into a welcoming, modern home designed for long-term owner occupancy.

### The Challenge

As an established development, the property was structurally sound but felt visually tired and dated. The original finishes lacked character, the lighting was uneven, and the spatial flow between the kitchen, dining, and living areas felt fragmented. The owner wanted a refreshed modern home, complete with new door installations, air conditioning upgrades, and a post-renovation cleanup, all within a strict budget of RM 30,000 and a swift 2-month timeline.

### Our Approach

We adopted a **Modern Contemporary aesthetic with warm neutral tones** — blending deep espresso cabinetry, warm wood fluted paneling, soft grey fabrics, and refined brass and gold accents to achieve a cozy yet luxurious feel.

Key interventions included:

- **Living area acoustic & feature wall**: Installed a full-height dark oak fluted panel feature wall that anchors the living room, complemented by warm recessed ceiling cove lights and a sleek black ceiling fan.
- **Dining room integration**: Enhanced visual connectivity between the kitchen and dining room with a large framed wall aperture, allowing light to flow through while serving as a functional visual anchor behind a 6-seater solid wood dining set.
- **Kitchen cabinet & surface makeover**: Refreshed dark panel kitchen cabinetry paired with quartz-style black countertops, blue-grey subway tile backsplash, and upgraded modern appliances.
- **Master suite enhancement**: Added elegant wall molding/wainscoting panels, a plush upholstered headboard with gold accents, and a large horizontal mirror to maximize natural light and visual depth.
- **Bedroom climate control upgrade**: Installed brand-new air conditioning units in two bedrooms to ensure energy-efficient cooling and optimal indoor comfort for restful nights.
- **Bathroom tile resurfacing**: Refreshed the bathroom walls using a two-tone epoxy tile paint coating — converting dated tiles into a crisp white and teal contrast without the cost and mess of full hacking. Fitted with modern black sanitary accessories, a new vanity counter, and a sleek instant water heater unit.
- **Door replacements & post-renovation cleanup**: Replaced 3 doors serving the yard and bathroom areas for improved privacy and modern aesthetics, concluding with a comprehensive deep-clean service of the entire unit prior to handover.

### The Result

Delivered on time within the 2-month window and right on the RM 33,000 budget, the refurbishment completely revitalized the space. The result is a refined, move-in-ready home that offers the owner high-end contemporary comfort every day.
