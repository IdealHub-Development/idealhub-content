### The Project Story

To help the owner overcome significant rental listing friction, this 1,150 sqft unit at Sunway Belfield underwent a complete soft furnishing and styling transformation. Initially offered as a partially furnished space, the property struggled to attract prospective tenants in a highly competitive market where renters demand move-in convenience.

Because all structural fittings and built-in carpentry were already intact, our focus shifted entirely to soft furnishings, loose furniture selection, and cohesive interior styling within an efficient RM 35,000 budget. We adopted a warm Japandi-inspired aesthetic, introducing a plush neutral L-shaped sofa, warm wood coffee tables, textured area rugs, and floor-to-ceiling drapes to anchor the spacious living area. 

In the master suite, soft ambient lighting, clean white hotel-quality bedding, and curated decorative accents complement the existing fluted panel headboard and built-in vanity nook. Storage credenzas and minimalist artwork were strategically placed across entryway and dining zones to create distinct, inviting spaces. Executed smoothly within a strict 1-month timeline without requiring any messy hacking or wet works, this targeted styling project successfully elevated the property from an ignored partial-furnished listing into a premium, turnkey home ready to command top-tier rental rates.
