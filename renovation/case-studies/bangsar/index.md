## The Project Story

This executive condominium in Bangsar was structurally sound but aesthetically fatigued. The 1,500 sqft unit had good bones — high ceilings, large windows, and a practical layout — but years of tenant turnover had taken their toll. The landlord wanted a renovation that would attract quality long-term tenants and justify premium rental rates.

### The Challenge

Bangsar is a mature, family-oriented neighbourhood popular with Malaysian professionals and expatriates alike. The unit needed to feel like a **home, not a rental** — warm, durable, and thoughtfully designed. The previous fit-out had peeling paint, loose cabinet hinges, leaking plumbing fixtures, and an outdated electrical system that was both inefficient and potentially unsafe.

### Our Approach

We targeted **Malaysian professional families** as the primary tenant demographic. The design direction was **Contemporary with warmth** — a neutral, timeless palette with rich textures, functional zones for family life, and materials that withstand the wear and tear of children and daily use.

Key interventions included:

- **Full M&E overhaul**: Complete electrical rewiring with strategic plug point additions, dedicated air-conditioning circuits, LED track lighting in the living area, and new fan installations in all bedrooms. Full plumbing inspection and rectification — leak mitigation, water heater installation, and sanitary fixture replacement
- **Custom carpentry throughout**: Built-in entertainment unit and TV console in the living room, floor-to-ceiling wardrobes in all three bedrooms, a fully fitted dry kitchen with soft-close cabinetry, and a dedicated study nook with integrated desk and shelving
- **False ceiling and lighting design**: New plaster ceiling throughout concealing exposed conduits and beams, with layered ambient, task, and accent LED lighting creating distinct zones for relaxation, dining, and work
- **Premium painting and wall treatment**: Full Nippon Paint Satin-Glo specification with dedicated sealer coat, two finishing coats, and comprehensive surface rectification — patching, skim-coating, sanding, and anti-fungal treatment in moisture-prone areas
- **Rental-grade materials**: E0 melamine boards for all carpentry (lowest formaldehyde emission), high-pressure laminate flooring rated for heavy traffic, and moisture-resistant kitchen cabinetry

### The Result

The unit achieved a **+19% rental yield uplift** and secured a three-year tenancy agreement with a Malaysian professional family within one week of listing. The landlord's return on renovation investment is projected at just 16 months.
