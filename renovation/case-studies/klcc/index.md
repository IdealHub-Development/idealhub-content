## The Project Story

This 500 sqft serviced apartment in KLCC was transformed from an outdated rental into a high-demand home for young professionals. We reimagined the entire space — opening up the living area, modernising the kitchen, and creating a calm, well-lit bedroom zone.

### The Challenge

The unit was dated with compartmentalised rooms, dark finishes, and inefficient use of space. Located in a prime KLCC address, it was underperforming in the rental market despite its excellent location. The landlord was achieving below-market yields and experiencing longer vacancy periods between tenancies.

### Our Approach

We applied our data-driven demographic analysis to identify the target tenant profile: young urban professionals working in the KLCC area. The design direction was **Urban Minimal** — clean lines, neutral palette, smart storage, and a sense of spaciousness despite the compact footprint.

Key interventions included:

- **Open-plan reconfigured layout**: We removed non-structural partitions to create a flowing living-dining-kitchen zone that maximises natural light and creates the illusion of a larger space
- **Custom modular cabinetry**: Floor-to-ceiling built-in wardrobes and a fully integrated kitchen with soft-close hardware, maximising every square foot
- **Premium yet durable finishes**: E0-grade melamine boards, high-pressure laminate flooring, and Nippon Paint EasyWash series for long-term maintainability
- **Smart lighting design**: Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting with warm 3000K LED throughout to create a welcoming atmosphere

### The Result

The unit now commands **RM 700 more per month** in rent with **zero vacancy** since listing. The transformation achieved a **+22% rental yield uplift**, paying back the renovation investment within 14 months of tenancy.
