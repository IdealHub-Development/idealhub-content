## Before You Commit

**__What renovation package is right for me?__**  
We offer three service categories to suit different renovation needs:

- **Essential Rental Setup** — Ideal for new or vacant units that need essential fittings such as lights, fans, air-conditioning, curtains or blinds, and bathroom accessories before renting out.
- **Minor Renovation & Refresh** — Suitable for existing homes that need professional painting, minor repairs, touch-ups, or practical upgrades before moving in or between tenancies.
- **Complete Renovation** — Best for owners who want a fully renovated, furnished, and move-in-ready or rental-ready home, including space planning, built-in carpentry, feature works, furniture, and optional appliances.

**__What types of properties do you renovate?__**

We primarily renovate residential properties, including serviced apartments, condominiums, landed homes, SOHO units, and studio apartments.

We also take on selected small commercial projects, such as office painting, basic fixture installation, and minor renovation works, subject to scope and availability.

**__How long will my renovation take?__**

The timeline depends on the scope of work, site condition, material availability, and building management approval.

- **Essential Rental Setup:** From around 2 weeks
- **Minor Renovation & Refresh:** From around 2 weeks
- **Complete Renovation:** From 1 month onwards

We will provide a project timeline before work begins and keep you updated throughout the renovation.

**__Do you take on small renovation projects?__**  
Yes. We can assist with anything from minor repairs and a room refresh to a complete home makeover.

**__Can you work within my budget?__**  
Yes. Share your preferred budget, and we will recommend the most suitable package and prioritise the works that matter most.

**__How do I get a renovation quotation?__**

Send us your floor plan, photos or videos of the unit, and a brief description of what you need. If available, include your budget and target completion date. We will prepare a clear quotation based on the agreed scope.

## Scope & Process

**__What does a Complete Renovation include?__**

Depending on your needs and budget, a Complete Renovation may include:

- Space planning and design
- Electrical, plumbing, lighting, and painting works
- Ceiling and feature-wall works
- Custom carpentry, such as kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, TV consoles, and storage
- Furniture, curtains or blinds, bathroom accessories, and optional appliances
- Final styling and handover

Your final scope will be clearly stated in the quotation.

**__What is the renovation process?__**

Our process is simple:

1. **Share your requirements -** Send us your floor plan, unit photos or videos, renovation needs, budget, and target start date.
2. **Confirm the scope and quotation -** We prepare the quotation based on your floor plan and photos. For existing homes with built-ins to upgrade or replace, a site visit may be needed before the quotation is finalised.
3. **Renovate and hand over -** Once confirmed, we finalise the details, coordinate the renovation works, provide progress updates, and hand over the completed home.

**__Do I need approval from my building management?__**

For condominiums and serviced residences, renovation approval and site rules are usually required. We will guide you through the required submissions and work according to your building’s regulations.

**__Can I stay in the home while renovation work is ongoing?__**

For minor touch-ups or small repair works, this may be possible. For larger renovation projects, we recommend vacant possession to ensure safety, smoother progress, and better work quality.

## Payment & Warranty

**__What is the payment schedule?__**

Payments are made in stages according to project progress:

- **40%** upon confirmation
- **30%** at the agreed project milestone
- **20%** upon completion of the remaining works
- **10%** upon final handover

The payment milestones and amounts will be stated clearly in your quotation or agreement.

**__What warranty do you provide?__**

All renovation works come with a **3-month workmanship warranty**. Materials and appliances are covered by their respective manufacturer warranties.

**__What happens if I find defects after handover?__**

We provide a **7-day defect-liability period** after handover for minor touch-ups related to the completed scope of work. Please notify us within this period, and our team will review and arrange the necessary rectification where applicable.

**__Are there additional charges after I confirm the quotation?__**

Any work outside the agreed scope—including additional requests, changes to materials, or site conditions not identified earlier—will be discussed and quoted separately before work proceeds. No additional work will be carried out without your approval.
