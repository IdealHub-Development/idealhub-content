## Before You Commit

**What packages do you have?**
We offer 3 tiers. Refresh: cosmetic updates (RM 5k-15k). Renovate: full interior (RM 15k-40k). Transform: structural + design (RM 40k+). Every package is customised to your unit.

**How long does renovation take?**
Refresh: 1-2 weeks. Renovate: 3-5 weeks. Transform: 6-12 weeks. All come with a fixed timeline guarantee.

**Can I visit a completed unit?**
Yes. WhatsApp us your address and preferred date. We arrange a site visit within 48 hours.

**How do I get a custom quote?**
Send us 3 photos of your unit via WhatsApp. You'll receive a detailed quotation within 24 hours - no commitment needed.

**What's the smallest unit you renovate?**
Studio apartments from 300 sqft. No project is too small.

## Pricing & ROI

**How is the renovation cost calculated?**
Cost is based on your unit size, scope of work, and material selection. Our digital platform generates an itemised quote so you see exactly where every ringgit goes — no hidden fees, no surprises.

**Do you offer payment plans?**
Yes. We offer milestone-based progress payments at 30%, 30%, 30%, and 10% upon handover. Renovation loan facilitation is also available through our partner banks.

**What ROI can I expect?**
Our clients average a +17% rental yield uplift post-renovation. The renovation typically pays for itself within 12-18 months of tenancy through higher rental rates and reduced vacancy periods.

**Is there a warranty on your work?**
Yes. All renovation work comes with a 24-month workmanship warranty. Materials carry their respective manufacturer warranties. We also provide a 7-day defect liability period for touch-ups after handover.

## Scope & Process

**What does your turnkey renovation include?**
Our full scope covers: electrical fittings & wiring, plumbing & piping, false ceiling & ambient lighting, custom carpentry (wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, TV consoles), professional painting with premium Nippon/Jotun paints, appliance coordination, and property defect profiling. We handle everything — you handle nothing.

**What's the renovation process from start to finish?**
1) Submit your unit details via WhatsApp or our instant quote form. 2) Receive a digital quotation within 24 hours. 3) Site visit & measurement within 48 hours of approval. 4) Track progress via your landlord dashboard with real-time photo logs. 5) Final walkthrough & handover. The entire process is managed through our digital platform.

**Do I need council approval (MPKJ/MBSA/MBSJ/MBPP)?**
For cosmetic renovations (Refresh tier), no approval is needed. For Renovate and Transform tiers involving structural changes, we handle the entire council submission process on your behalf — including architectural drawings, Form B submissions, and liaison with the management office.

**Can you work while my tenant is still in the unit?**
No. We require vacant possession for safety and efficiency. However, our fixed timeline guarantee means we complete within the agreed schedule so your unit is back on the market as quickly as possible.
