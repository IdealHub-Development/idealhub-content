This **280 sqft studio at Astrum Ampang** — unit **A-15-05** — was completed into a rental-ready home within an **RM 11,500 all-inclusive budget** covering feature wall finishes, a kitchen extension, curtains, bathroom fittings and the full appliance package. Because the unit already had its core furniture in place, the fit-out was deliberately targeted: budget went into the finishes and appliances tenants notice first, and everything was delivered as a single turnkey handover.

The living zone is anchored by a **wainscoted feature wall** behind the TV, finished with professional painting and paired with a floating timber console for display and storage. Full-length **double-track curtains** — a warm taupe main drape layered over a sheer — soften the full-height window and frame the city view, while the same panel moulding continues behind the bed for a consistent, hotel-inspired language. The compact plan still holds a queen divan with an upholstered headboard, a two-door wardrobe, a bedside table and a dining set for two without ever feeling crowded.

The kitchen was extended from the existing cabinet run with **melamine base and wall cabinets** topped by a **quartz stone countertop**, and equipped with a Faber electric hood and ceramic cooker, a Toshiba two-door refrigerator and a Hisense 9kg/6kg washer dryer combo. A 50-inch Google TV was wall-mounted and cabled, and the bathroom was finished with a storage mirror, towel rack, shampoo holder and frosted glass treatment — a fully equipped, move-in-ready rental package built for fast tenant acquisition at **RM 1,500/month**.
