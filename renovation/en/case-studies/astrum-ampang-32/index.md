This **280 sqft studio at Astrum Ampang** was transformed into a modern, move-in-ready rental within an all-inclusive **RM 17,140 budget** — covering custom feature accent walls, kitchen cabinetry, furnishing, and complete appliances. To maximize investor returns, the fit-out was executed as a complete turnkey package delivered together for a hassle-free handover.

The open-plan layout is anchored by a **dark vertical wood slat TV feature wall** paired with a matching wood-slat study nook backdrop. The sleeping area pairs a **grey upholstered queen divan bed** with full-height layered main and sheer curtains for a refined, hotel-inspired ambiance. The kitchen extension adds clean white and taupe-grey cabinetry topped with a **quartz stone countertop**, complemented by a built-in induction cooker, washer dryer combo, standing black refrigerator, and wall-mounted Smart TV — creating a high-yielding, fully furnished rental package built for rapid tenant acquisition.
