Perched high in the Kampung Bharu enclave of Kuala Lumpur, this 3-bedroom unit at Legasi Kampung Bharu was transformed into a warm, contemporary homestay designed to showcase the best of the city. Executed through expert turnkey renovation services in Kuala Lumpur within a 2-month timeline, the project delivers a fully furnished, move-in-ready home that balances everyday comfort with an unforgettable skyline view.

The living area is the heart of the home — a bright, open-plan space dressed in soft neutrals with taupe accent walls and crisp white wainscoting. A grey sofa and marble-look rug anchor the lounge, while the adjacent dining nook, framed by a round mirror and pendant lighting, flows seamlessly into a fully fitted white kitchen with integrated cooking appliances.

Each of the three bedrooms is fully furnished with upholstered bed frames, hotel-quality bedding, and thoughtful storage — the master bedroom with a queen bed and a large city-view window, and the third bedroom with a single bed ideal for a child or guest. Throughout, black ceiling fans, track lighting, and air conditioning keep the space comfortable. The standout feature is the floor-to-ceiling balcony window, offering a sweeping view of the Petronas Towers and the Kuala Lumpur skyline — a memorable detail that elevates this homestay beyond a typical rental. This project shows how tailored renovation services near Kampung Bharu can create a distinct, high-appeal stay for guests.
