## The Project Story

Sanderling Lakefront Residence located at Cyberjaya, this condominium was transformed into a serene and personal **contemporary classic** sanctuary for the owner's own stay. Designed to balance everyday comfort with timeless elegance, the home was thoughtfully crafted within an RM65,000 renovation budget.

Soft cream wainscoting frames the hallway, dining area, and bedroom walls, introducing structural sophistication and visual warmth throughout the home. Upon entry, custom-built cabinetry with a sleek black display niche sets a refined tone while offering practical storage. In the main dining area, beveled diamond-grid accent mirrors flanked by classical wall moulding enhance the sense of space and light. The master bedroom pairs subtle wall paneling with a gold-trimmed upholstered bed for a luxurious retreat, while built-in mirrored wardrobes maximize spatial efficiency across the secondary bedrooms.
