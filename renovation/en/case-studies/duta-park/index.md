This **2+1 bedroom unit at Duta Park Residences** on Jalan Kuching, Kuala Lumpur, was fitted out end-to-end as a single turnkey package for **RM 43,300 all-inclusive** — covering feature wall joinery, a custom kitchen, both bedrooms, the living and dining areas, appliances and a final deep clean. Rather than a piecemeal upgrade, the scope was planned as one coordinated handover so the residence could be occupied immediately on completion.

The design language is deliberately warm and quiet. A **fluted oak batten panel** runs from the TV wall through to the divider between the living and dining zones, backed by textured stone-look panels behind the wall-mounted television, with a floating timber console below. In the living room a **round backlit LED mirror** softens the same wall, while floor-to-ceiling **double-track curtains with sheers** layer the windows in a taupe that carries through both bedrooms. Every room was repainted, and the lighting was rebuilt around **track bars and recessed downlights** so the evening mood is warm rather than flat.

The two principal bedrooms each received a **queen divan with a pocket-spring mattress**, side tables, canvas artwork and a mirrored wardrobe — six feet in the master, four feet in the second room — with the master headboard set against its own fluted panel lit by vertical LED strips. The **utility room was partitioned with an aluminium sliding door**, creating the "+1" room that makes the layout work as a three-bedroom. The kitchen is the most heavily engineered element: an **L-shaped run of oak base and cream wall cabinets** beneath a **quartz stone countertop**, with a single-bowl sink, two-zone gas hob, slim carbon-filter hood, a frosted window to borrow daylight, and a **washer dryer tucked under the counter** alongside the refrigerator and microwave — completing a fully furnished, move-in-ready home.
