Designed to maximize tenant appeal and long-term rental performance, this **2-bedroom, 1-bathroom** unit at **Anya @ Shorea Park, Puchong** was crafted into a warm, modern sanctuary. Completed on a tight, high-ROI budget of **RM24,000**, the project delivers a complete turnkey space—fully equipped with air conditioning in all rooms, custom built-in kitchen cabinetry, and stylish lighting fixtures.

The open-plan layout creates a seamless flow between the dining and living spaces, highlighted by a contemporary wave pendant light over a round wood dining set and framed accent moulding along the TV wall. The kitchen is outfitted with rich wood-grain cabinetry, black countertops, and a built-in hob, offering full cooking functionality for tenants. In the bedrooms, neutral tones paired with dark wood wardrobes and dedicated air conditioning ensure maximum comfort, making this unit a standout choice in the rental market.
