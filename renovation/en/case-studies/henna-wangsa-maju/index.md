This brand-new 2-bedroom unit at Henna Residensi in Wangsa Maju was set up turnkey for the rental market on an all-in RM 30,600 budget. From smart door lock to final photography, the project delivered a complete rental-ready home — furnishing, styling, appliances, and multi-platform listing all handled in one seamless package.

The interior adopts a clean Scandinavian modern language that appeals broadly to young professional tenants. The living area pairs a teal tufted sofa with a white low-profile TV console on a blue-grey accent wall, accented by a gold-leaf blossom canvas and geometric rug. A round light-wood dining table with green-upholstered chairs sits against elegant wainscoting, while the compact kitchen balances dark wood-grain uppers with matte taupe lowers and a white solid-surface countertop. Both bedrooms are finished with wood-look vinyl plank flooring, wainscoting feature walls, and complete bedding with queen and single divans. Every detail — curtains, track lighting, Daikin air conditioning, kitchen appliances, and bathroom accessories — was included, making this unit immediately rentable in Wangsa Maju's competitive market.
