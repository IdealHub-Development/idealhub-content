---
eyebrow: HANDOVERS
title: Move-in and move-out, handled.
subtitle: Every handover follows the same checklist — inspected, photographed and signed off, with the record kept against the tenancy.
moveInBadge: MOVE-IN
moveInSteps:
- Pre-tenancy inspection with dated photos of every room
- Keys, access cards and meter readings logged
- Inventory list signed by the tenant
- Deposit receipt and tenancy documents filed to the unit
- Cleaning and any pre-move-in repairs scheduled
moveOutBadge: MOVE-OUT
moveOutSteps:
- Joint inspection against the move-in photo record
- Damage assessment with quotes for any repairs
- Cleaning, painting and turnover works arranged
- Keys returned and access revoked
- Deposit deductions agreed and the refund statement issued
footnote: Both checklists are stored on the tenancy timeline in IdealHub Tenancy, so there is never a dispute about what the unit looked like.
