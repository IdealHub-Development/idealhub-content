title: Before you ask.
subtitle: Still unsure? Book a 20-minute walkthrough and we will show you the beta on your own units.
items:
- question: Is the beta really free?
  answer: Yes. No setup fee and no monthly charge while you are in the beta. Beta participants keep preferential pricing when we launch — we will spell it out before you agree to anything.
- question: Do I have to move my existing tenancies?
  answer: No. We import your existing units and tenancies with you during onboarding — the whole setup takes an afternoon.
- question: Does it handle LHDN e-stamping?
  answer: Yes. Stamp tenancy agreements without leaving the dashboard, and the certificate is stored on the tenancy record.
- question: Can your team run the tenancy for me instead?
  answer: Yes — that is our done-for-you plan. We onboard your units and handle agreements, e-stamping, rent tracking and renewals, with monthly statements and a named contact.
- question: What happens to my data if I leave?
  answer: Your data is exportable at any time — tenancy records, agreements, certificates and payment history. Nothing is locked in.
- question: Where is the offline service available?
  answer: The software runs anywhere in Malaysia. Our in-house crew currently covers Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley.
- question: Can you verify an applicant’s documents for me?
  answer: Yes. We screen applicants and verify documents on request — our team can call the employer, college or immigration office on your behalf.
- question: Is this a building management system?
  answer: No. IdealHub Tenancy is built for individual landlords, small portfolios and letting agents — not strata buildings, JMB/MC accounts or facility bookings.
- question: Does it work for a landlord with one or two units?
  answer: Yes. It is built for individual owners and small portfolios — one unit or twenty, the dashboard is the same.
- question: What is tenancy management software?
  answer: 'Software that runs the admin side of renting: agreements, e-stamping, rent tracking, renewals, deposits and handovers — in one place instead of spreadsheets and chat threads.'
