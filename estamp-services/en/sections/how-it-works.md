overline: How It Works
title: Stamp Your Document in 3 Steps
subtitle: >-
  No queues, no MyTax portal, no runs to the district land office. Upload
  once, pay online, and your official LHDN e-Stamp certificate arrives by
  email — usually within five minutes.
steps:
  - title: Upload your document
    description: >-
      Create an account in under a minute and upload your tenancy agreement
      or other eligible document as a PDF. We check the document type and
      contract value automatically, so you know immediately whether it can
      be stamped online.
  - title: Review and pay
    description: >-
      Stamp duty is calculated for you — RM50 for most tenancy agreements —
      and shown in full before you pay. Settle it securely online by credit
      card, FPX bank transfer, or e-wallet. No subscription, no hidden
      fees: you pay per document, only when you stamp.
  - title: Receive your certificate
    description: >-
      Your official LHDN e-Stamp certificate is issued instantly and
      delivered straight to your email with a unique certificate number for
      verification. Download it, file it, share it with your tenant — it is
      legally valid nationwide from the moment it is issued.
