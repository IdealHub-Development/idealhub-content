overline: SASSA 2026
title: The 30-Day Rule Is Now Enforced
subtitle: >-
  The Self-Assessment Stamp Duty System (SASSA) took effect on 1 January
  2026. Stamping is no longer optional — and late stamping carries real
  penalties.
paragraphs:
  - >-
    Under SASSA, stamping a tenancy agreement is the responsibility of the
    parties to the document, not a government counter. Every agreement must
    be stamped within 30 days of execution. Miss that window and the LHDN
    adds penalties on top of the duty, calculated from the date the
    document should have been stamped.
  - >-
    The same 30-day window is exactly why online stamping exists. Because
    you can stamp from your phone the day you sign, there is no reason to
    let a deadline slip — most IdealHub certificates are issued in under
    five minutes, less time than it takes to make a cup of kopi.
  - >-
    If unsigned or unstamped agreements have been sitting in a drawer, stamp
    them now. The penalty for stamping late is always higher than the RM50
    duty you would have paid on time.
penalties:
  - label: Within 30 days
    value: No penalty
    description: Stamp on time and you pay only the flat RM50 duty.
  - label: Up to 3 months late
    value: RM 50 or 10%
    description: >-
      A penalty of RM50 or 10% of the unpaid duty — whichever is higher —
      is added on top of the duty itself.
  - label: More than 3 months late
    value: RM 100 or 20%
    description: >-
      The penalty doubles to RM100 or 20% of the unpaid duty, whichever is
      higher, on top of the original duty.
note: >-
  Penalty tiers follow the Stamp Act 1949 as amended — IdealHub calculates
  them for you at checkout, so there are no surprises.
