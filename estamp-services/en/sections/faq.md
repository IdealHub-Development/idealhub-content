---
title: Frequently Asked Questions
items:
  - question: What is e-Stamping?
    answer: >-
      E-Stamping is the digital way to stamp a document under Malaysia's
      Stamp Act 1949. Instead of queuing at a district office or fighting
      with the MyTax portal, you upload your agreement online, pay the stamp
      duty, and receive an official LHDN e-Stamp certificate by email. It
      carries exactly the same legal weight as a stamp bought over the
      counter.
  - question: Do I need a MyTax or LHDN account?
    answer: >-
      No. IdealHub e-Stamps handles the entire LHDN integration on your
      behalf — you never have to register for, log in to, or navigate the
      MyTax portal. Create an IdealHub account, upload, pay, done.
  - question: Which documents can I stamp online?
    answer: >-
      Tenancy and rental agreements, service agreements valued under
      RM50,000, and employment contracts are all eligible. If your document
      cannot go through the self-assessment route, we tell you before you
      pay and point you to the right channel.
  - question: How long does it take?
    answer: >-
      Most documents are stamped in under five minutes. Your certificate is
      emailed to you the moment it is issued, carrying a unique certificate
      number you can use to verify its authenticity.
  - question: What happens if I miss the 30-day deadline?
    answer: >-
      Late stamping attracts a penalty: RM50 or 10% of the duty if stamped
      within three months of the deadline, and RM100 or 20% if stamped
      after three months — on top of the duty itself. If an agreement is
      already late, stamp it immediately so the penalty stops growing.
  - question: Is the certificate legally valid?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Certificates issued through IdealHub are official LHDN e-Stamp
      certificates issued via LHDN integration, and they are fully legally
      valid and enforceable in Malaysia. Every certificate carries a unique
      number that can be verified against LHDN records.
