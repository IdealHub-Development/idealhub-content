## tv-guide

**1. TURNING ON / OFF**
- Press power button on remote control to turn on/off the TV

**2. CHANGING CHANNELS**
-  Use buttons on remote control to control. Alternatively, you can
manually key in channel number using the number button (0-9).

## washing-machine

**1. Power**
Tap to turn the washing machine on and off.
- If you do not use the control panel for 10 minutes after turning the washing machine on, it will turn itself off automatically.
- This washing machine turns itself off after the washing cycle is complete.


**2. Navigation dial**
Turn the navigation dial to the left or right to navigate through the cycles and functions. As you turn the navigation dial the screen will display your selection.
